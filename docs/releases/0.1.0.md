The first release of **Album Artwork**, a small native macOS app that puts the cover of the song playing in Apple Music or Spotify on your desktop as a 164 × 164 pt rounded tile.

## Highlights

- **Apple Music and Spotify.** Whichever player is playing is shown. If both are idle, the last shown player stays.
- **Keeps the last cover** when you pause, stop or quit the player, and restores it after relaunching the app.
- **Drag anywhere** on the cover to move it. Near another desktop widget or a screen edge, an outline previews where it will snap.
- **Remembers** its position, visibility and last cover across launches, including on multi-display setups.
- **Menu bar menu** (also on right-click): Launch at Login, Show/Hide Artwork, Reset Position, Quit.
- **Missing artwork** shows a neutral music-note tile and retries with backoff.
- **Private by design:** Apple frameworks only, no Dock icon, no account, API key, server or analytics. Spotify covers are fetched only from Spotify's own image CDN over HTTPS.

## Requirements

- macOS 14 Sonoma or later
- Apple silicon or Intel (universal binary)

## Install

1. Download `Album-Artwork-0.1.0.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Move **Album Artwork.app** to `/Applications`.
3. This build is ad hoc signed and not notarized, so macOS will block the first launch. Either right-click the app and choose **Open**, then confirm, or run:
   ```sh
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Album Artwork.app"
   ```
4. Play a song and approve the Automation prompt for Music and/or Spotify.

## Known limitations

- Not notarized (see Install).
- Shown on desktop Spaces only, not in full-screen app Spaces, and not in macOS's Edit Widgets gallery.
- Rebuilding or updating the app may make macOS ask for Automation permission again.
